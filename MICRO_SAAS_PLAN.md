# Micro-SaaS Plan: AI Voice Receptionist Demo Builder

## The Idea

A free tool where anyone can create a live AI voice demo for their business in 2 minutes.

## How It Works

1. User goes to our website
2. Enters their business name and type
3. Records or types their greeting
4. Gets a link to a live AI voice demo they can call
5. Shares it with customers, posts on social media

## Monetization

### Free Tier:
- 1 demo, 10 calls/month, our branding

### Pro Tier ($29/month):
- Unlimited demos, 500 calls/month, custom branding
- Analytics dashboard
- Lead capture

### Custom Build ($497 one-time):
- Full AI receptionist setup for their business
- Custom voice, scripts, integrations
- 30-day support

## Why This Works

1. **Viral loop**: Every demo has our branding, drives traffic back
2. **Lead generation**: Free users become paying customers
3. **Low cost**: Vapi free tier + Supabase free tier + Vercel free tier
4. **Fully automated**: No human interaction needed to deliver value
